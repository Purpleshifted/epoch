**Verification status**
- ✓ = the DOI resolves on Crossref and the title matches the citation (`tools/research/check_dois.py`, 25 of 25 DOIs). This confirms the *reference exists*, not that the claim in the table is what the paper says. The claims (temperatures, rates, percentages) come from search-tool summaries and are unread.
- [?] = could not be verified; do not cite as-is.
- [I] = interpretation, not fact.
- Evidence grade: **A** peer-reviewed field study documenting formation; **B** peer-reviewed but indirect, single-study, or classification only; **C** review or book only; **D** press only / unverified.

## 요약 (Korean executive summary)

"인공물과 자연 지반이 특정 조건에서 만나면 새로운 혼성 물체가 태어나 지층에 남는다"는 규칙을 학술적으로 방어하기 위한 문헌 표입니다.

- **문헌이 가장 탄탄한 사례**: 플라스티글로머레이트(Corcoran 외 2014, 모닥불 열), 파이로플라스틱(Turner 외 2019), 플라스티크러스트(Gestoso 외 2019, 파도 마모), 인류기원 퇴적암 anthropoquinas(Fernandino 외 2020, 시멘트화), 슬래그 암석화(Owen 2025, 수십 년), 알바트로스 보러스(Young 2009).
- **분류·개념 문헌**: 테크노솔(WRB 2022), 기술화석(Zalasiewicz 2014, 2017), 만들어진 지반(Ford 2014).
- **약하거나 확인 불가**: "Carson et al. 2013 plasticrust"는 확인되지 않았으므로 인용 금지. 폐어망–산호 얽힘의 구체 문헌, 트리니타이트의 동료심사 출처도 미확인.
- **게임 매핑**: 이 모델에는 파도, 태양풍, 기름이 없습니다. 화재는 선택적 이산 이벤트로 두고, 파도 마모는 보행 횡단(trampling) 부하로 대체합니다. 이 대체는 [I] 해석이며 문헌이 직접 지지하지 않으므로 심사에서 "대리변수"라고 명시해야 합니다.
- **시간**: 8시간 = 100만 년이면 1초 ≈ 34.7 모델 연도. 문헌 속 형성시간(며칠~수십 년)은 모두 실시간 1초 이하로 압축됩니다. 규칙의 근거는 절대 시간이 아니라 조건과 시간의 *순서*(즉시 < 수년 < 수십 년 < 수백 년)에 둡니다.
- **상위 규칙(증거 × 시각 명료성)**: 플라스티글로머레이트(화재), 보러스(새+플라스틱), 파이로플라스틱, 플라스티크러스트, 다크어스(숯+뼈+음식물).

## 2. Game ruleset

**Clock.** 8 h = 1,000,000 yr is a *log-compressed* clock (`geoClock.ts`: years = (1+t)^1.3456 − 1). The linear average would be about 34.7 modelled yr per real second, but the actual rate runs from ~1.3 yr/s at the start to ~36 yr/s at 4 h. Checkpoints: 10 s ≈ 25 yr, 100 s ≈ 500 yr, 1 h ≈ 61,000 yr.

**Literature timescales do not constrain game seconds.** Every formation time in the literature (minutes to ~35 yr) would collapse to under about a second. The defensible claim is the *ordering*: instant < years < decades < centuries. [I] Game thresholds are chosen for visibility and dramaturgy, and that choice must be stated explicitly.

**Proxies, all [I].**
- Trampling crossings stand in for wave abrasion.
- A fire event stands in for a campfire or open burning (not in the model today; an optional discrete event).
- Burial depth or time since deposit stands in for diagenesis or cementation.

**Extras, not full rules.**
- Plastisessile: plastic fibre plus shell stock gives shell wrapped in threads (Ellrich 2023).
- Mummified food: food waste plus burial gives a brown dried lump and newspaper (Rathje, grade C).
- SCP layer and microplastic horizon: stratigraphic markers (Swindles 2015; Rose 2015; Brandon 2019). Render as glitter in strata, not as sprites.

## 3. Conceptual references (fact versus interpretation)

All content claims below come from background knowledge or search summaries; the books were not read.

1. **Zalasiewicz et al. 2014** (doi:10.1177/2053019613514953 ✓). Fact: defines technofossils as preservable remains of the technosphere. [I]: supports treating each hybrid as a stratigraphic marker.
2. **Haff 2014** (Geol. Soc. Spec. Publ. 395:301–309, doi:10.1144/SP395.4 ✓; Crossref title "Technology as a geological phenomenon: implications for human well-being", issued 2013 online). Fact: introduces the technosphere as a geological system. [I]: justifies treating the technosphere as an agent, not only as human intent.
3. **Corcoran & Jazvac 2020** (doi:10.1038/s43017-019-0010-9 ✓). Fact: proposes plastiglomerate as a marker horizon. [I]: the clearest "contact produces an object neither side chose" case.
4. **Parikka 2015**, *A Geology of Media* (University of Minnesota Press). [I]: media, minerals and waste form one deep-time material chain.
5. **Haraway 2016**, *Staying with the Trouble* (Duke University Press). [I]: "making-with" supports hybrids as joint products.
6. **Tsing 2015**, *The Mushroom at the End of the World* (Princeton). Fact: "contamination as collaboration" is a key concept. [I]: frames hybrids as collaborations.
7. **Yusoff 2018**, *A Billion Black Anthropocenes or None* (University of Minnesota Press). [I]: a critical counterweight; asks whose extraction made the "natural" ground.
8. **Bennett 2010**, *Vibrant Matter* (Duke). [I]: material agency. Not searched; verify the citation.
